FV About Me

The person behind the pixels 🙋‍♀️

Hi, I’m Feba.

A UI/UX designer and frontend developer, working with AI to turn ideas into websites, product interfaces and practical automation. I enjoy shaping the details, seeing them work, and learning what to improve next.

About me

I enjoy the point where an idea starts becoming something I can see and use. Sometimes that means refining the position of a card or the pace of an animation. Sometimes it means stepping back and asking why a task needs to be repeated at all.

My work spans design and frontend development, with AI supporting how I explore and build. I define the direction, guide the implementation and review the result. Working this way has made me more deliberate about explaining what I want and more patient with the iterations needed to get there.

I also enjoy connecting people around a project. On the directory, I coordinated the design and digital output while working with stakeholders, backend developers and support. Being able to take responsibility for the whole design while relying on other people’s expertise is something I’m proud of.

How I work with AI

Earlier, I sometimes went straight to AI hoping it would give me an idea I had not yet worked out myself. I spent time asking for something new without being clear about what I wanted. That experience changed my process.

Now I begin with research, often using AI to explore the topic and identify questions or gaps. I use that understanding to develop my own direction and workflow before asking Claude Code to build. Then I review the output, explain what needs to change and keep refining it.

The better I understand the problem and my preferences, the more useful AI becomes. Learning to direct it has been as important as learning what it can produce.

What I’m learning next

I want to keep finding better ways to organise my work and involve users more consistently in evaluating it. Reviewing a design myself helps me refine its appearance and behaviour; feedback from people using it helps me understand whether those choices work in practice.

The learning journey

  1. 01
    Exploring motion — turning an animation idea into a working website.
  2. 02
    Designing with intention — giving a product a clearer direction before asking AI to implement it.
  3. 03
    Improving the workflow — using experience of manual production to build a repeatable system.

Let’s talk

I’m looking for a role that brings together design, frontend development and AI automation — a team where I can take ideas into implementation, learn from others and keep improving how the work gets done.
